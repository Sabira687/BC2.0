THE IDEA

The best teacher

AIGO is a collection of digital items. Each card has a story and a power: the Key teaches, the Lock tests, the Compass helps you mid-task. Paste a card into the AIGO lesson or into your own Claude, and the AI becomes your coach for that skill. Collect the set, earn the stamps, keep every card.
